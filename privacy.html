Holomint Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Optume Ventures LLC ("Optume," "we," "us") handles information in connection with Holomint at holomint.app and related applications (the "Service").

Holomint is privacy-first by design: your collection, portfolio, tracked cards, cost basis, and settings are stored locally on your own device and are never sent to us. We cannot see them. This policy describes the limited information that is nonetheless involved in operating the Service and in billing for Holomint Pro.

1. The short version

  • No account required. No login, name, or email is needed to use Holomint's free tier.
  • Your collection data stays on your device, in your browser's local storage. We do not receive it, store it, or have any ability to read it.
  • We do not use advertising, and we do not use cross-site tracking. (See Section 5. This assumes no analytics have been added.)
  • We never see your card number. Holomint Pro payments are handled by a third-party payment provider acting as merchant of record.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Loading the Service and card images necessarily reveals your device's IP address to our hosting/CDN providers and to third-party image hosts, as with any website.

2. Information stored on your device (not collected by us)

The following is stored in your browser's local storage, on your device only: - your tracked cards, collection, and portfolio; - purchase prices / cost basis you enter; - fee, channel, and currency settings; - cached price and live-drop data; - your Holomint Pro license key, if you have one.

This data is not transmitted to Optume. You can delete it at any time from within Holomint, or by clearing your browser's site data for holomint.app. Because it lives only on your device, we cannot recover it for you if you lose it, so please back it up.

3. Information we or our providers process

  • Technical/hosting logs. Holomint is delivered via GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. These providers automatically log standard technical data (IP address, browser and device type, timestamps) for delivery, security, and abuse prevention. This is standard for essentially every website. We do not use these logs to build profiles.
  • Live drops feed. Holomint retrieves publicly available restock/drop information from our Cloudflare-hosted server component. This is a one-way retrieval of public data. We do not send your collection, portfolio, or personal data to that server.
  • Third-party card data and images. Prices and images are sourced from third parties (including TCGplayer data via TCGcsv). When images load, your browser requests them from third-party servers, which will receive your IP address and standard request data. Their content is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Support communications. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to respond.

4. Holomint Pro: payment and subscription data

Holomint Pro is a recurring monthly subscription. Payments are processed by Polar (the "Payment Processor"), which acts as the merchant of record. - You provide payment details directly to the Payment Processor. Optume does not collect, receive, or store your full payment card number. - The Payment Processor handles billing, recurring charges, payment security, and applicable sales tax/VAT. - We receive limited order and subscription information, such as: the email address you provide at checkout, confirmation that a purchase or renewal occurred, subscription status (active / cancelled / lapsed), the license key issued to you, and country/region for tax purposes. - We use this to deliver and validate your license, provide support, honor cancellations and refunds, and keep required business and tax records. - The Payment Processor's own privacy policy also applies: https://polar.sh/legal/privacy.

5. Cookies and local storage

Holomint uses your browser's local storage for functional purposes only, to save your collection, settings, and license so the app works. This is necessary/functional storage, not advertising or cross-site tracking, and Holomint does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

Verified: as of this version, Holomint's source contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no third-party tracking scripts of any kind. (Confirmed by scanning the application source, not merely assumed.)

If any analytics is ever added, including self-hosted or "privacy-friendly" analytics, this policy must be updated before it ships, and users in the EU/UK will likely require a consent mechanism. See COMPLIANCE_MAINTENANCE.md.

6. Third parties we rely on (sub-processors)

When you use Holomint, your browser contacts the following third parties. Each receives, at minimum, your IP address and standard request information. This is inherent to how the web works.

Provider Purpose What it receives
GitHub (GitHub Pages) Website hosting IP address, request data
Cloudflare CDN, DNS, and our drops-feed server IP address, request data
jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) Delivers the OCR library used by the card scanner IP address, request data (only when the scanner is used)
Pokémon TCG API (api.pokemontcg.io) Card search and lookup IP address and the search terms you type
Polar (Polar Software, Inc.) Payment processing / merchant of record Your checkout email, payment details, billing country
TCGplayer / TCGcsv Card price and product data Price data is bundled with the app; no direct request from your browser

Fonts are self-hosted. The typefaces Holomint uses are served from holomint.app itself. Your IP address is not sent to Google or any font network to render this app.

The card scanner runs entirely on your device. It uses on-device optical character recognition. Photos and camera frames are never uploaded, transmitted, or sent to us or to any third party. The scanner only downloads the OCR software library from jsDelivr; your images stay local. (If we ever change this, for example by adding a cloud image-recognition service, we will update this policy and tell you before it ships.)

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, we process the limited data described above on the basis of: contract (to provide and bill for Holomint Pro), legitimate interests (to secure and operate the Service and prevent abuse), and legal obligation (to keep tax and transaction records).

8. Your rights and choices

Because your substantive data lives on your own device, you already have direct control: view, edit, export, or delete it in Holomint at any time.

For the limited data we or our providers hold (support emails, subscription/order records), depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent, for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

If you are a Holomint Pro subscriber, Polar's customer portal also lets you download a complete export of your personal data, subscriptions, and orders in one click.

To make any other request, email mmmilliard@optumeventures.com. We may need to verify your request (for example, by matching the email used at checkout).

9. Children's privacy

Holomint is a tool for collectors, investors, and sellers and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the applicable age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Holomint Pro may only be purchased by adults (18+). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact mmmilliard@optumeventures.com and we will delete it.

10. Retention and security

Data on your device persists until you delete it. Data held by our providers (hosting logs, subscription and tax records) is retained per their policies and as long as needed for business, security, accounting, and legal purposes. We use HTTPS in transit. No system is perfectly secure; the local-first design substantially limits what is at risk, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

We operate from the United States and use providers that process data in the United States and elsewhere. If you use the Service from outside the U.S., the limited technical and billing data described above may be processed in the U.S.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the version and date above and, where appropriate, by additional notice (email and/or in-app).

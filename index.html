Market prices track Near Mint. Other grades price at a percentage of NM. The percentages below are typical dealer numbers; tap one to change it, and every single in the app follows. When live condition pricing is switched on, cards use real per-condition market prices instead, and the chip reads live; these percentages remain the offline fallback.
